# September 14 production capacity review

This historical assessment is superseded by the dedicated per-category workers in [README](README.md).
Snapshot: September 13, 2026, 19:33–19:38 UTC. This is a current-backlog assessment,
not a monthly traffic forecast. Future traffic growth and incremental power costs
have not been measured.

All physical queues already have registered consumers. Keep the three topic workers,
one article worker, pooled AI worker, two background workers and solver. Dedicated
source, relationship and verification deployments remain at zero because the pooled
AI worker covers them; adding consumers cannot repair exhausted source jobs or
increase the shared provider allowance.

Notifications had 134 eligible queued deliveries, oldest approximately three hours,
while both background workers were busy with extraction. They completed 348 deliveries
in the preceding 15 minutes, but old ready work remained. Enable one dedicated
notification worker using the existing v0.0.11 image, delivery credentials and queue.
This adds a request of 100m CPU and 256Mi memory (1Gi limit), and takes worker pods
from eight to nine. It does not release new application code or add AI demand.

After Fleet convergence, verify ready/zero restarts, registered notification queue
coverage, successful deliveries and falling eligible queue depth/age. Keep this worker
while extraction can starve delivery. Reassess for scale-down after a representative
busy period with no delivery backlog, rather than after one empty sample.

Database capacity also constrains scaling. The shared PostgreSQL primary has
max_connections=160. The DevFeed session-mode PgBouncer has default_pool_size=10
per pool and max_client_conn=60. A snapshot found 117 client backends cluster-wide,
14 for DevFeed. API pools currently retain one connection per process; increasing
every pool indiscriminately can pin the pooler's server connections and move waiting
downstream. Reporting isolation and query optimization are being tested in the app
repository; their deployment remains on hold. Before increasing interactive pools,
budget replicas, process count, workers, report refreshes, rollout surge and failure of
one pooler instance together. Measure wait time at both ORM and PgBouncer layers.

Dodo Payments Blog and Figma Blog each exhausted three source-enrichment attempts.
Both are failed, not queued, so additional source workers would not resolve them.
Their generic saved ValueError does not establish editorial irrelevance.
